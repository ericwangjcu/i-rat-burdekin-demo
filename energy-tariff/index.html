How the tool is used

  1. Enter client, account and pump details.
  2. Upload monthly MyAccount or 30-minute Ergon data.
  3. Check fallback assumptions where detailed values are missing.
  4. Compare eligible tariffs using the historic period.
  5. Review the recommendation with the client.
  6. If the client changes tariff, record the change date.
  7. Upload new monthly data and confirm the tariff remains cheapest.

Step 2 of 7

Load the client's electricity usage data

Use the best data available. Monthly MyAccount data is enough to start; detailed 30-minute data gives a stronger assessment.
